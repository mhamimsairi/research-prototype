01 · introduction

The AI layer inherits the fragmentation it sits on

Malaysian digital government promises citizen-centricity — MyDIGITAL, AI-RMAP, NAIAP 2026–2030, MD2030 all commit to it — yet services remain organised around agencies: LHDN, SOCSO, JPN, JPJ operate as separate systems behind a common portal. The AI layered on top is predominantly chatbots that retrieve text but cannot verify it. General-purpose LLMs hallucinate on 58–88% of verifiable legal questions; even purpose-built retrieval tools err on 17–33% (Dahl et al., 2024; Magesh et al., 2025).

02 · literature review

Three capabilities. No system on record has all three.

Capability 1
Computable eligibility
Computed against SHACL shapes, not retrieved as pre-written text (Konstantinidis et al., 2025).
Capability 2
Proactive delivery
"No-Stop Government": offered before the citizen asks.
Capability 3
Cross-agency chaining
Once-Only: one agency's document is valid evidence elsewhere (Karunarathne et al., 2022).
SHACL + PROV-O — never combined before

SHACL answers did the output conform? PROV-O answers where did it come from? Recording each validation as a provenance activity yields a continuous, queryable chain.
